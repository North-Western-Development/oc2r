# Tablet
![Not a stele](item:oc2r:tablet)

Tablets are portable [computers](../block/computer.md). You can take them with you inside of your inventory.

Once started, tablets will keep running until they run out of power, or you turn them back off. This also means you can put them inside of a different inventory or toss them, and they'll keep running!

### Configuration
Configure the components in a tablet in its inventory screen. To access this screen switch to the inventory screen using the toggle button to the left of the terminal screen.

### Energy
Tablets feature a sizable battery. To recharge a tablet, it is recommended to make use of the [charger](../block/charger.md). Just place a container (like a chest or barrel) on top of a powered charger, and place the tablet inside.

### Connectivity
No devices can be externally connected to the tablet. In order for the tablet to communicate with other computers, make use of [network tunnel cards](network_tunnel_card.md).
